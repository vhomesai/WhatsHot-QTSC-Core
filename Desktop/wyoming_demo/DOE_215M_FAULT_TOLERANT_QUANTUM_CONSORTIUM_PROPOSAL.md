## 2. Project Abstract & Scientific Merit

Triqee Labs and IMQbd 501(c)(3) propose to establish **standardized, physically verifiable utility benchmarks** for DOE quantum testbeds (Oak Ridge National Laboratory, Brookhaven National Laboratory, Argonne National Laboratory) utilizing non-equilibrium quantum hydrodynamic quench dynamics.

While static randomized benchmarks fail to measure macroscopic coherence under non-local Hamiltonian evolution, our published benchmark (**DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23045297**) directly observes **Kardar-Parisi-Zhang (KPZ) superdiffusion ($z = 1.52 \pm 0.04$) across 100 superconducting transmons** on a heavy-hexagonal lattice (840 native CZ gates), demonstrating where classical Matrix Product States (MPS, $\chi \le 128$) reach an **entanglement wall at $t^* \approx 2.12\,\hbar/J$**.

## 3. Key Technical Deliverables for the DOE Grand Challenge

1. **Scalable Utility Testbed Compilation (100 to 1,000+ Qubits)**:
   - Provide DOE national lab users with a verified Qiskit 2.x pass manager compiling 1D and 2D Bethe-ansatz integrable Hamiltonians onto sparse lattice QPUs without SWAP overhead.
2. **Biomolecular Hydrodynamic Solvation**:
   - Model hydrophobic droplet dissolution and cancer-associated p53 R273H phase separation across 64+ transmons, bridging quantum hydrodynamics with structural biology.
3. **Open-Science Hardware Telemetry Repository**:
   - IMQbd 501(c)(3) will maintain an open-access, vendor-neutral hardware benchmark repository publishing calibration drift, Pauli error syndroming, and $T_1/T_2$ coherence scaling across IBM Heron, Rigetti, and DOE testbed processors.
